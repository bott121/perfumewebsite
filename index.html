ROQS- A Lifestyle Brand

“

After a hard day’s work pushing through Mumbai local trains in my first job, the end of day would be so dreadful and exhaustive.

It was my neighbor who noticed my plight and gifted me a perfume with the thought that not only could one begin the day fresh but also hopefully end it too with the same feeling of freshness.

The gift was significant in many aspects, it made me introspect about my own body odor and the need to feel and smell good. No turning back since, and thereafter not a day in my life have I begun without wearing a perfume on me.

More than three decades since that day and I have used many brands. To my own surprise I have still treasured the half sparingly used first perfume bottle gifted to me, reminding me of my early days.

Glad now I have my own roqs family brand of distinct fragrances which could change the way many others could look at life just as I did.

The ROQS fragrances are well chosen and crafted to suit one’s need to feel good. The ROQS perfume holds the distinction of being affordably priced thus catering to all segments in comparison to international brands whose top, middle and base notes are mostly similar to ours. To summarize the perfume in a single phrase would be “ROQS Beyond Senses”

In this venture I am ably supported by my spouse and two soccer passionate kids, hence the social responsibility of passing on part of the proceeds to fund youngsters in our very own AIFF affiliated ROQS Football Club.

Do try out our ROQS Perfumes!

Oswin D'Souza

Founder-ROQS Beyond Senses LLP
